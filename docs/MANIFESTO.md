# Dartio Manifesto

Dartio is the most satisfying way to play, score, learn, and compete at darts from any screen. It should feel immediate at the oche, credible to serious players, and inviting to someone trying darts for the first time.

## Ideal

- Accurate rules and companion scoring for X01, Cricket, Around the Clock, Shanghai, Count-Up, and focused practice modes.
- AI opponents from level 1 through 20 with transparent, progressively stronger accuracy and believable throw distributions.
- Fast local and online friend play with reconnectable, server-authoritative matches.
- A visual dartboard that records, explains, and replays every dart while keeping scores and turns synchronized.
- Hands-free scoring through always-on voice mode with explicit listening/processing/accepted/error states and correction controls.
- A dynamic checkout helper that shows best routes, professional alternatives, setup shots, bogey warnings, and player-tailored paths.
- A fair, production-ready membership system with clear value, Stripe billing, self-service account management, and no dark patterns.
- A rich, striking landing experience and game UI that works at mobile, tablet, and desktop sizes using Navi UI `v1.0.0`.

## Non-goals

- Reusing any legacy Dartio code.
- Shipping fake multiplayer, fake AI levels, fake checkout logic, or decorative-only voice controls.
- Treating responsive design as desktop compression.
- Activating real-money production billing before transaction and webhook proof passes.
