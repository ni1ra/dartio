## Gaps, ranked

### 1. The match does not fit the design centre on a phone

The spec's load-bearing assumption is one phone on a stool. At 390×844 the match
page is **1882 px tall** — 2.2 viewports. The first screen is navigation, leg
strip, scoreboard, and dartboard; every scoring input sits below the fold. A
player passing the phone around scrolls to score, scrolls back to read the score,
and repeats it every visit.

### 2. The segmented control has no visible selected state

Measured on `/play` at 1440×1000: the container reports `border-width: 0px` and
`background: rgb(5, 6, 7)` against a `rgb(9, 10, 10)` canvas, and **both**
options compute to `color: rgb(247, 247, 244)` with transparent backgrounds.
"Who are you playing?" therefore renders as the flat run-on text
"Dartio AI Local Friend" with nothing to show which is chosen. Navi UI ships a
correct pill treatment for this component; `src/app/globals.css` is unlayered, so
its rules beat Navi's layered ones wholesale. The same control drives the
Board / Visit total / Each dart switch inside the match.

### 3. Eight of nine catalog rows are not games

`/practice` lists Cricket, Around the Clock, Shanghai, Count-Up, Bob's 27,
Checkout Lab, Doubles Matrix, and Scoring Sprint. All eight are `href="#"` and
labelled "COMING NEXT". Only X01 is playable. There is also no mode abstraction
to add them against: `x01-match.tsx` hardcodes X01 throughout.

### 4. A match cannot survive a reload

There is no serializer or rehydrator. Match state lives in one `useState` in
`x01-match.tsx`; a refresh, a backgrounded tab that gets evicted, or a dropped
connection loses the leg. Nothing about this is visible to the player before it
happens.

### 5. Six tables are defined and never used

`rooms`, `room_members`, `matches`, `players`, `turns`, and `darts` exist in
`src/db/schema.ts` and are applied through migration `0005` on both Neon
branches. No code reads or writes any of them — the only tables in use are
`users`, `profiles`, `subscriptions`, and `webhook_events`. So there is no match
history, no statistics, no cross-device continuity, and no room state, despite
the schema and the marketing surface both promising them.

### 6. Online play is a dead end that advertises itself as live

`/friends` offers "Host a room", disabled, still labelled **"coming in Cycle 2"**
— stale, since Cycle 2 is closed. "Join a friend" accepts a six-character code,
shows "Finding room…" for a hardcoded 700 ms `setTimeout`, then always fails with
"That room isn't live." The page then lists "Server-authoritative matches",
"Reconnect without losing a turn", and "Spectator-ready event history" as
though they exist.

### 7. Correction only reaches the latest dart

`undoLastDart` is the whole correction model. A dart recorded two visits ago, a
misheard voice score, or a wrong aggregate total cannot be repaired without
restarting. There is no event log to replay from, which is also what blocks
gap 4 and gap 5.

### 8. Twenty AI levels share one aim policy

`chooseAiAim` is bull at 50, direct low doubles, T20 otherwise. Only the miss
radius changes across levels 1–20. A level 3 opponent and a level 19 opponent
make identical decisions and differ only in how badly they execute them, so the
ladder does not read as twenty distinct players.

### 9. "Always-on" voice is a single clip

The voice lifecycle records one 4.5-second clip, transcribes it, and waits for a
manual resume. There is no voice-activity detection, no silence segmentation, no
automatic re-arming, and no queued correction. `confirm` and `cancel` are parsed
but not applied as control dialogue.

### 10. The checkout companion talks nonsense at the start of a leg

At 501 with three darts in hand it prints "No valid 3-dart route is available
from 501." That is literally true and useless — it is the normal scoring phase,
not a failure. The panel needs a scoring-phase voice distinct from its
no-route voice.

### 11. Visit history spends a desktop column on nothing

At 1440×1000 the history rail is a fixed 310 px column that is entirely empty
until the first visit lands, then shows at most six rows. It is the third of
three columns in the match grid while the scoring inputs are compressed into the
middle one.

### 12. Fonts are fetched with a render-blocking CSS import

`src/app/globals.css` line 1 is an `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/…')`
for three families. That serializes a third-party stylesheet ahead of first
paint, on a product whose spec promises twelve seconds from page load to first
dart on a phone. Next.js ships `next/font` for exactly this.

### 13. Dead theme definitions shadow the live ones

`globals.css` defines the silver and blood token sets twice: once under
`[data-theme="…"]` and once under `[data-navi-theme="…"]`. `NaviProvider` stamps
`data-navi-theme`, and `document.querySelector('[data-theme]')` returns null, so
the first copy never applies. Editing it produces no visible change. The themes
themselves were re-verified as working: app-styled elements flip correctly
between black and silver.

### 14. The account hub shows membership and nothing else

`/account` renders plan, access state, cancellation, and entitlements. It does
not show match history, statistics, rooms, or practice progress, because none of
those are persisted (gap 5).

### 15. There is no browser test harness in the repository

Every viewport, geometry, and theme proof recorded in `docs/` so far was produced
by an ad-hoc external session and cannot be re-run by CI or by the next person.
The repo has `vitest` and nothing else; the dartboard regression gate named in
`REPO_CONTROL.md` has no executable form.

### 16. Observability, analytics, and rollback are undocumented

`PHASE_1_v1_foundation.md` still has "Implement observable error handling,
analytics events, and rollback paths" open. There is no error reporting, no
analytics events, and no documented rollback beyond "Vercel retains prior ready
production deployments."
